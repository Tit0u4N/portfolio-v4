#### **Project Overview**
**Onlympic Odyssey** is a management, 4X exploration, and adventure game inspired by the Olympic Games. This project was developed over five months with two teammates as part of the **Games On Web** tournament, where it secured **3rd place**. The game draws inspiration from *For the King* and *Baldur’s Gate III* and is built using *BabylonJS* to provide an immersive 3D experience.

#### **Game Concept**
Players take control of a team of athletes navigating a **hexagonal board**. The objective is to accumulate as many points as possible by winning competitions, culminating in a **final event simulating the Olympic Games** after four years of training.

#### **Core Mechanics**
- 🏋️ **Training Centers** – Improve athletes' skills.
- 🏟️ **Arenas** – Host competitions where players can earn items and progress.
- 🏆 **Tournaments** – Award points based on performance, leading to ultimate victory.

#### **Technology Stack**
The game is built with a **modern and efficient stack**:
- 🧊 **3D Graphics** – *BabylonJS* for immersive environments.
- 🖥️ **User Interface** – *React* for a smooth and interactive experience.
- 🎨 **Design & Styling** – *NextUI*, *Tailwind*, and *SCSS* for a polished visual presentation.
- 💻 **Programming Language** – *TypeScript* for structured and maintainable code.
- ⚡ **Build & Performance** – *Vite* for fast and optimized development.
- ✅ **Testing & Code Quality** – *Jest*, *Prettier*, and *ESLint* to ensure stability and readability.
